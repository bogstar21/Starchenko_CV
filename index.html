StarX — Last Mile Ecosystem

StarX is a multi-tenant logistics and field management SaaS that bridges enterprise databases with field operations. Built with Node.js, Express, Supabase, and Stripe, it allows drivers and operators to log real-time check-ins (GPS, photos, commentary) via a lightweight PWA or a simplified Telegram bot without complex app onboarding. The platform includes automated PDF proof-of-visit generation, a multi-tenant manager dashboard, REST APIs, and strict data isolation designed for high reliability in low-connectivity environments.
